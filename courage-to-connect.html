How the program runs

The program is six 2-hour group sessions held weekly, followed by a two-night weekend retreat in the beautiful Gold Coast Hinterland. The retreat includes further group sessions, and plenty of time to experience peace and stillness as well as connection with the rest of the group.

To help you relax, you will have your own private cabin with ensuite, a relaxing massage, and home cooked lunches and dinners.

Why a group?

Our earliest relationships shape the ways we learn to connect with other people.

When those relationships have involved trauma, inconsistency, rejection, criticism or a lack of emotional safety, we can carry these experiences into our adult relationships, often without realising it.

A therapeutic group offers something individual therapy cannot fully replicate: the opportunity to notice and work with relationship patterns as they happen, in real time.

Within the group, you can begin to:

  • Recognise patterns in the way you relate to others

  • Understand how your past experiences influence your relationships today

  • Receive thoughtful, respectful feedback from others

  • Practise expressing yourself, setting boundaries and responding differently

  • Experience being seen and accepted without having to hide parts of yourself

  • Develop greater confidence in creating safe and fulfilling relationships

The aim is not simply to talk about relationships.It is to experience a different kind of relationship, and practise taking that experience into your life outside the group.

What we will explore

  • Understanding complex trauma

    Learn how experiences of trauma can influence emotions, relationships, beliefs, nervous-system responses and patterns of behaviour.

  • Coping skills

    You will learn emotional regulation skills, distress tolerance strategies, and other ways to effectively manage painful thoughts and feelings.

  • The interpersonal process

    Together, we will pay attention to what is happening between group members. You may begin to notice familiar relationship patterns emerging and have the opportunity to explore them safely.

  • Real-time feedback

    The group provides a unique opportunity to hear how others experience you, while also learning how your responses affect the people around you.

  • Effective interpersonal skills

    Develop practical skills for communication, boundaries, emotional regulation, conflict and building trust.

  • Understanding your internal parts

    Explore the different parts of yourself that may have developed to protect you, cope with difficult experiences, or help you navigate relationships.

  • Group EMDR

    During the retreat, there will be opportunities to engage in EMDR using a structured, trauma-informed group intervention: G-TEP (Group Traumatic Episode Protocol). This can be used to support healing and integration, through the safe processing of traumatic memories that continue to negatively affect you.

A space to practise something new

You don't need to arrive knowing how to connect.

You don't need to have relationships figured out.

You don't need to be perfectly comfortable in a group.

The group is a place to slow down, become curious about yourself, and experiment with new ways of relating.

With careful facilitation, the group becomes a place where you can practise being more open, communicating more honestly, receiving feedback, repairing misunderstandings and discovering that connection can be safe.

The Courage to Connect journey

  1. Understand

    Learn how trauma and early relationships can shape the way you connect.

  2. Notice

    Become aware of your patterns, protective responses and internal parts.

  3. Explore

    Bring curiosity to what happens within you and between you and others.

  4. Practise

    Experiment with new ways of communicating, connecting and responding.

  5. Connect

    Build greater capacity for safe, authentic and fulfilling relationships.

From connection within the group to connection in your life.

The hope is that the relationships and skills developed within the group become a foundation for healthier, more connected relationships beyond it.

Courage to Connect is an invitation to take the next step, with support, curiosity and courage.

Is Courage to Connect right for you?

This group may be worth exploring if you:

  • Have experienced complex or developmental trauma

  • Often feel lonely or disconnected from others

  • Find relationships difficult despite wanting meaningful connection

  • Have difficulty trusting or letting people get close

  • Notice recurring patterns in your relationships

  • Struggle with boundaries, conflict or communicating your needs

  • Want to understand yourself more deeply in relationship with others

  • Are ready to gently practise new ways of relating

A suitability and screening process will help determine whether this group is an appropriate fit for you.

Meet your facilitator

RA

Rachael Ambler

Clinical Psychologist | EMDR Consultant

Rachael Ambler is a clinical psychologist with more than 25 years of clinical experience and is an EMDR consultant. She is also the current convenor of the APS EMDR Special Interest Group.

Rachael is Director of Abundant Life Clinical Psychology, a private practice in Brisbane, and she has been developing and facilitating private therapeutic groups since 2010.

She believes strongly in the unique capacity of a group of people to provide connection, acceptance and transformation. Courage to Connect grew from Rachael's experience of seeing many clients who deeply long for connection and belonging, while simultaneously finding it difficult to develop relationships that feel safe, reciprocal and fulfilling. This group has been specifically developed to address that gap.
