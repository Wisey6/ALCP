Courage to Connect is a small therapeutic group program for healing complex trauma through connection and relationships.

Facilitated by clinical psychologist Rachael Ambler, the program brings together weekly group sessions and a two-night retreat in the Gold Coast Hinterland.

Meet your facilitator

Rachael Ambler

Clinical Psychologist · ALCP

Rachael is the owner and sole practitioner at Abundant Life Clinical Psychology. She has been practising as a psychologist since 1999 and in her own private practice since 2002.

She has experience facilitating interpersonally oriented groups for adults and advanced training in evidence-based treatments for people who have experienced trauma.
